Agent Infra Architecture

Public overview of the governed collaboration runtime.

Safety boundary: the public runtime defaults to dry-run operation, requires explicit approval for side effects, and records evidence for independent verification.

Evidence-backed lifecycle

1. PlanNormalize the request and declare the acceptance contract.
2. ExecuteRun bounded, allowlisted work with durable progress evidence.
3. VerifyCheck observed state independently of executor claims.
4. ReviewRetain the evidence bundle and record the final outcome.

Design principles

  • Fail closed on malformed input, unsafe actions, and missing evidence.
  • Keep domain-agent responsibilities separate from infrastructure operations.
  • Make retries, restart adoption, and rollback observable and idempotent.
  • Keep production writes disabled unless explicitly authorized.
